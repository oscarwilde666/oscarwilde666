I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at **Fudan University**, advised by [Prof. Xin Wang](https://faculty.fudan.edu.cn/wangxin/zh_CN/). My research interests include **AI agents**, **large language models**, **human-computer interaction**, and **AI for education**.

I previously worked as a Research Assistant at the Digital World with Intelligent Things (DOIT) Lab at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I received my M.Eng. from Beijing Institute of Technology and B.Eng. from Beijing Jiaotong University.

## Research interests

- Long-horizon AI agents, tool use, memory, and task orchestration
- Foundation models, reasoning, alignment, and evaluation
- Human-centered AI and personalized learning systems
- RFID sensing and multimodal 3D scene understanding

## Selected work

- **StudyHarness** — a long-horizon knowledge-management and review-agent system.
- **Reasoning-model alignment platform** — modular rollouts, rewards, policy optimization, and evaluation.
- **Transformer LM training stack** — a from-scratch implementation of core language-model training components.
