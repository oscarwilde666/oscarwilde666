# Ziang Wang

Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/) · Jointly trained at [Zhongguancun Academy](https://www.bza.edu.cn/)

Research Intern at [EISOO (壹索智能)](https://www.eisoo.com/), leading the design and development of [SciLand](https://sedna.medgeai.cn/).

**Research:** Data-Centric AI · Agentic Systems · Post-Training · AI4Science

I study how execution feedback and improvement trajectories can be turned into reliable data and training signals for improving AI agents.

## Selected Systems

- **Curve-SWE / DistillWheel** — failure-grounded data production for coding agents.
- **SciLand** — a persistent, evidence-grounded biomedical R&D workspace designed to turn research episodes into candidate capabilities for qualification, validation, reuse, and revision. It connects research planning with laboratory-facing interaction and supports cross-episode capability evolution.
- **Agentic Data** — natural-language-driven data preparation for LLMs with the [DCAI Lab](https://github.com/OpenDCAI).
