I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at **Fudan University**, jointly trained by the National AI School and Beijing Zhongguancun Academy. I am advised by [Prof. Xin Wang](https://faculty.fudan.edu.cn/wangxin/zh_CN/), Party Secretary of the College of Computing and Intelligence Innovation, and I am also a Research Intern at **壹索智能**.

My research focuses on **Data-Centric AI**, **data synthesis**, **agentic data and harness systems**, **post-training**, and **AI4Science**. I am interested in turning development-time feedback and measurable improvement trajectories into reliable training signals, and in building scientific agents that accumulate reusable capabilities across long-running R&D workflows.

## Research

- **Feedback-grounded data synthesis**: requirement-grounded task construction, improvement trajectories, and attributable SFT, preference, and RL signals.
- **Agentic data and harness systems**: evaluation designs that separate visible development feedback, hidden generalization, and terminal outcomes.
- **LLM post-training systems**: GRPO-family methods, off-policy optimization, rollout reuse, and training-stability analysis.
- **AI4Science workspaces**: persistent biomedical R&D environments that extract, register, and reuse scientific skills and workflows.

## Core Projects

- **Curve-SWE**: feedback-grounded data synthesis for SWE-agent post-training, with requirement-level evaluation and measurable software-improvement trajectories.
- **SciLand**: a self-evolving AI-native workspace that turns biomedical R&D experience into reusable scientific skills and workflows.
- **LLM Training Systems**: an end-to-end PyTorch stack spanning decoder-only Transformer pretraining, vLLM rollouts, GRPO-family objectives, and off-policy optimization.
